Nikan.lv

A company website for a Riga based firm that installs and services commercial refrigeration and air conditioning equipment. It presents the company's services, about information and contacts, and has a product catalog with categories, subcategories and product pages. Catalog content and the about page gallery come from a Strapi CMS backend. The site is available in Latvian, English and Russian.

Features:
* Animated splash screen and hero with key company figures
* Services overview for refrigeration, air conditioning and maintenance
* Product catalog with categories, subcategories and product pages loaded from Strapi
* About page with mission, benefits and a project gallery
* Contacts page, sitemap and full company requisites in the footer
* Three languages with locale based URLs and SEO tags

Tech: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, GSAP, i18next, React Router, Strapi 5 (backend)

Admin panel (Strapi CMS):
The site content is managed in a Strapi CMS admin panel. It has a content manager for products and categories, a content type builder, a media library, and users and roles. Shots 10 to 16 show this panel.
