POP MARKET (Funko POP Marketplace)

An online store for Funko Pop collectible figures with a dark storefront. Shoppers browse the catalog by category, filter rare and exclusive figures, keep a wishlist and pay through Stripe Checkout. Admins manage products, orders, categories and customers from their own dashboard.

Features:
* Catalog with categories, search and filters for grails, exclusives and new releases
* Product pages with condition, box number, edition and stock status
* Cart stored in Redis and checkout through Stripe with webhook order fulfillment
* Customer accounts with order history and a wishlist
* Admin dashboard with revenue, orders, low stock alerts and product, order, category and customer management
* Product editor with image upload and flags like Grail, Exclusive, Vaulted and Trending

Tech: Next.js 16 (App Router, server actions), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Prisma 7 with PostgreSQL, Redis, Auth.js v5, Stripe, Framer Motion
