Desktop app (Windows)

A desktop launcher and store for a software catalog. The left panel splits products into owned and available. Each product page shows a media carousel with a trailer, a status badge, last update date, tiered pricing by duration (day, week, month) and buy buttons for add on options. A Node.js and Prisma backend serves the catalog, product data and uploads.

Features:
* Product catalog split into owned and available items
* Product page with media carousel, pricing tiers and add on purchases
* Status and membership badges per product
* Account menu and per product changelog
* Backend with product database, image uploads and admin seeding

Tech: C++ desktop client, Node.js, Prisma, ImGui style UI
